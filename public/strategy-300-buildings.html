💡 Performance-Only Model

After the 1st month probation, stipend is strictly tied to performance. If a scout onboards fewer than 6 buildings, they drop back to probation pay. This keeps the team hungry and filters out non-performers fast — you are NOT carrying dead weight.

📋 Scout Daily Workflow

  1. Morning Briefing (9:00 AM) — Check assigned region for the day. Review target buildings list on the PropX Scout Dashboard (a simple Google Sheet or Notion board). Pick 10–15 buildings to visit.
  2. Field Visits (9:30 AM – 1:00 PM) — Door-to-door cold visits. Talk to watchmen first to get the owner's number. If owner is present, give a 5-minute live demo of PropX on their phone. Log every visit with status: Interested / Not Interested / Follow-Up / Onboarded.
  3. Lunch Break + WhatsApp Follow-ups (1:00 – 2:00 PM) — Send WhatsApp messages to "Interested" owners from previous days. Share PropX brochure PDF + video link.
  4. Afternoon Visits (2:00 – 5:00 PM) — Continue field visits. Focus on owners who were not available in the morning. Target commercial areas, PGs, hostels, and large apartments.
  5. Evening Report (5:00 – 5:30 PM) — Submit daily report: number of visits, leads generated, demos given, buildings onboarded. Report via WhatsApp to Region Lead.

🗺️ Region Mapping Strategy

Before deploying a single scout, you need intelligence. Map every high-density residential zone in your target cities.

📍

Step 1: Identify Hotspots

Use Google Maps to identify areas with dense apartment buildings (3+ floors, 15+ flats). Mark them on a shared Google My Maps layer.

  • Search for "apartments near [area]"
  • Cross-reference with 99acres/MagicBricks listings
  • Look for rental yield areas (near IT parks, colleges, hospitals)
  • Mark PG clusters — they have the most pain
🔲

Step 2: Grid Assignment

Divide each city into grids of ~2 km². Assign one scout per grid. Each grid should have a minimum of 50 potential buildings.

  • Scouts "own" their grid — full accountability
  • No two scouts overlap
  • Leaderboard by grid (gamification)
  • Weekly grid review with satellite view
📊

Step 3: Building Database

Before visiting, pre-build a database of all buildings in the grid using local intelligence, Google Maps pins, and broker contacts.

  • Building name, address, estimated flats
  • Owner name (if known), phone number
  • Visit status: Not Visited → Visited → Demo → Onboarded
  • Simple Google Sheet per region, shared with team

🎯 The Perfect Pitch — What Scouts Say at the Door

The 60-Second Elevator Pitch

"Sir/Madam, I'm from PropX — we are a free property management app that helps building owners like you track rent collection, electricity bills, maintenance, and generate receipts automatically. You have [X] tenants? Right now you're probably tracking everything in a diary or WhatsApp. With PropX, every tenant gets their own portal, payments are tracked in real-time, and you get a full financial dashboard. Thousands of owners across India are already using it. Can I show you a 2-minute demo on my phone?"

⚠️ Key Rules for Scouts

Never mention pricing upfront. PropX is free for owners right now — that's your biggest weapon. Lead with "free" and "automatic". Never argue with a reluctant owner — leave your card, follow up in 3 days. The demo sells itself.

Channel Partner Opportunities

Beyond your own scouts — leverage existing distribution networks that already touch building owners daily.

🏗️

Real Estate Brokers

Every city has 100+ active property brokers who personally know building owners. They can introduce PropX during their regular property dealings.

  • Offer ₹500–1,000 per onboarded building
  • Give them a branded referral link
  • Monthly leaderboard with prizes
  • They get a "PropX Certified Partner" badge
🔧

Property Maintenance Companies

AMC providers (lift maintenance, generator, plumbing) visit buildings weekly. They can mention PropX to owners during service calls.

  • Leave PropX pamphlets at every serviced building
  • Co-branded "Smart Building" certification
  • Revenue share on premium features (future)
  • They get leads from your owner network too
📺

Cable TV & Internet Providers

Local cable and broadband operators serve every apartment building. They already have billing relationships with building owners.

  • They literally enter every building monthly
  • Cross-promote: "Use PropX, get 1 month free internet"
  • Bundle PropX premium with internet plans (future)
  • Revenue share on cross-sold services
🏦

Chartered Accountants

CAs handle tax filings for building owners. PropX's ledger/receipt data is exactly what CAs need. Position PropX as a CA's best friend.

  • Export-ready financial reports for CAs
  • CA recommends PropX to all property-owning clients
  • Partner with CA associations in each city
  • Offer bulk onboarding sessions
🏠

Housing Society Federations

Many cities have housing society federations that represent hundreds of buildings. One presentation = access to 50+ buildings.

  • Present at federation monthly meetings
  • Offer free "Digital Property Management" workshop
  • Federation endorsement = instant trust
  • Co-host events with federation leadership
🏪

Paint / Hardware Shops

Building owners regularly visit local paint and hardware stores. These shopkeepers have direct relationships with 50–100 owners each.

  • Place PropX standees in shops
  • Shopkeeper gets ₹300 per referral
  • Minimal effort — just hand out cards
  • Trust-based: owner trusts their local vendor

Social Media & Content Strategy

Build authority, generate inbound leads, and create FOMO among building owners — all organically.

⚠️ Important: Never Reveal Owner Financials

Owners are extremely private about their rent, tenant details, and income. Never show real dashboards with actual numbers. Always use demo/mockup data in any content. Violating this destroys trust instantly.

🎬 Content Pillars — What to Post

🎥

Pillar 1: Founder Journey Reels

Personal reels of you and co-founders visiting buildings, pitching to owners, celebrating milestones. Raw, authentic, no filters.

  • "Day 1 of visiting 50 buildings" vlog
  • "Owner said YES in 2 minutes — here's how"
  • "We just hit 100 buildings!" celebration reel
  • Behind-the-scenes team huddles
💡

Pillar 2: Owner Pain Point Content

Educational content that makes owners think: "This is exactly my problem!" — then position PropX as the solution.

  • "5 mistakes landlords make with rent collection"
  • "Why WhatsApp is killing your rental business"
  • "How to never chase a tenant for rent again"
  • "Diary vs Digital: which landlord are you?"
📊

Pillar 3: Product Demos (With Fake Data)

Screen recordings of PropX in action using demo accounts. Show the dashboard, receipts, ledger — never real data.

  • "Generate 20 receipts in 10 seconds"
  • "See who hasn't paid — in one click"
  • "Send WhatsApp reminders to all tenants"
  • "Your entire building finances — one screen"
🗣️

Pillar 4: Owner Testimonials (Careful)

Only with explicit owner permission. Show their face + quote, but NEVER their dashboard. Focus on their emotional experience.

  • "I used to spend 2 hours on rent — now 2 minutes"
  • Simple selfie video with the owner (30 sec)
  • Text-overlay quote cards (if camera-shy)
  • Google Review screenshots (anonymized)

📍 Hyperlocal WhatsApp Marketing

The most underrated channel. Building owners live on WhatsApp — not Instagram.

  1. Create area-wise WhatsApp Channels — "PropX Owners — Jubilee Hills", "PropX Owners — Gachibowli". Owners join their locality's channel. Post updates, tips, and success stories here.
  2. WhatsApp Broadcast Lists — Every scout builds a broadcast list of owners they've met. Send weekly "PropX Update" messages with new features, tips, and social proof.
  3. Owner Community Group — A single group for your top 50 most engaged owners. They help each other, share experiences, and organically recruit their peers. You moderate.
  4. QR Code Stickers — Print QR code stickers that link to PropX signup. Scouts paste these on building notice boards (with permission). Cost: ₹2 per sticker.

GWD Ecosystem Cross-Selling

Leverage onboarded buildings as a distribution network for GWD Global, GWD Sports, and future ad revenue.

🔑 Core Insight

Every building you onboard is not just a PropX customer — it's a captive audience of 15–50 residents who live, work, and spend money in that locality. This is an advertising and distribution goldmine for the entire GWD ecosystem.

🌐

GWD Global — Freelancing Platform

Tenants and owners in onboarded buildings are potential users of GWD Global's freelancing marketplace and skill courses.

  • Promote skill courses to young tenants (college students, working professionals)
  • Offer building owners freelancer access for property-related work (design, accounting, legal)
  • Cross-promote via PropX tenant portal notifications
  • Run "Skill Workshop" events in building common areas
🏆

GWD Sports — Academy Management

Building residents are potential sports academy enrollees. Target children and young adults living in PropX buildings.

  • Promote sports academies to families in buildings
  • Offer "PropX Resident" discounts at GWD Sports academies
  • Host inter-building sports tournaments (visibility + engagement)
  • Building notice board posters for academy enrollment
📢

Future: Digital Billboards on Buildings

With owner permission, install small digital display boards in building lobbies or exteriors. Generate ad revenue from local businesses.

  • Phase 1: Simple poster boards (₹500/building)
  • Phase 2: LED screens in lobbies (₹5K–10K/building)
  • Revenue split: 60% PropX, 40% owner
  • Advertisers: local restaurants, gyms, coaching centers

GWD Global Academy — Courses & Events

Host skill-building events targeting building residents. Position GWD as the ecosystem that powers their entire life — home, career, fitness.

  • Free webinars promoted via PropX tenant portal
  • In-building workshop events (weekend skill camps)
  • Certificate courses promoted on building WhatsApp groups
  • "Learn & Earn" freelancing bootcamps for tenants
